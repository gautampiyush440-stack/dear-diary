Help & Support ❓

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my diary secure?

Yes, all your logs, entries, snaps, and configurations are stored purely locally on your device's browser localStorage. No data ever leaves your device.

How do I unlock other companions?

Maintain your daily reflections writing streak for 7 days to unlock Luna, Bruno, Finn, Pip, and Stella in the Character Studio!

How does Time Capsule work?

You can write a letter to your future self and choose a delivery date. The capsule will lock securely with a wax seal, and only open on that exact day.
